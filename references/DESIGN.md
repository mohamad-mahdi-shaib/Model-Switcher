## Brand & Style

This design system draws inspiration from spatial computing optics, precision glasscraft, and high-end photographic lenses. It balances ethereal transparency with definitive physical presence.

### Personality & Values
- **Optical Precision:** Elements feel physically ground and polished, possessing weight, refractive index, and specular response rather than digital abstraction.
- **Purity & Lucidity:** Uncluttered layouts bathed in soft illumination where content sits suspended in clear optical medium.
- **Calm Mastery:** Interactions mimic delicate changes in light incidence—gentle chromatic dispersion, surface reflections, and soft luminous responses that reward precision touch.

### Aesthetic Movement
Modern Luxury Glassmorphism grounded in physical optics:
- Translucent spatial layers with tuned transmission rates rather than milky, muddy opacities.
- Subtle chromatic refraction rims exhibiting cold cyan-to-indigo wavelength dispersion along perimeter edges.
- High-contrast, pristine dark charcoal typography ensuring legible hierarchy through luminous, fluid backgrounds.

## Colors

The palette simulates sunlight interacting with precision-polished optical glass. Surfaces maintain near-neutral transmissive clarity, accented by spectral refractions and grounded by deep mineral inks.

### Primary Spectrum (Refraction & Specular)
- **Primary Refraction (`#0e7490` - Deep Cyan):** Simulates the primary refractive fringe of dense crown glass. Used for interactive focus, active indicators, and edge refractions.
- **Secondary Dispersion (`#6366f1` - Spectral Indigo):** Acts as the secondary dispersion wavelength, used for gradient rim transitions, energetic callouts, and ambient highlights.
- **Prism Tint (`#38bdf8` - Light Caustic Cyan):** Applied as thin directional lighting accents and inner glow highlights.

### Glass Translucencies & Canvas
- **Pearlescent Base Canvas:** Crisp alabaster canvas (`#f8fafc` through `#f1f5f9`) modulated by ambient atmospheric blurs (`#e0e7ff` at 20% opacity and `#cffafe` at 18% opacity) positioned behind glass panes to activate their refractive qualities.
- **Glass Plate Fill (Base):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` on elevated planes; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` on floating layers.
- **Glass Hover Fill:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`.
- **Active / Pressed Fill:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.

### Typography & Structure
- **Charcoal Ink (`#0f172a`):** Primary text and definitive icons, guaranteeing contrast ratios above 12:1 against glass surfaces.
- **Slate Secondary (`#334155`):** Subtitles, meta-labels, and muted icons.
- **Muted Glass Tertiary (`#64748b`):** De-emphasized timestamps, inactive toggles, and placeholder tokens.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes high optical clarity. The interplay between **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** provides geometric balance without visual weight that competes against refractive glass edges.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric curvatures that mirror the rounded radii of polished lens elements. Tight tracking creates cohesive, sculpture-like text blocks.
- **Body Text (Inter):** Rigorously engineered neutral grotesque offering tall x-height and clear counterforms, delivering sharp readability across translucent glass planes.
- **Data & Precision Counters (JetBrains Mono):** Utilized for metadata badges, calibrated values, status codes, and coordinate markers, framing the UI with scientific precision.

### Rules
- Never use pure black (`#000000`). Always render primary copy in Deep Charcoal (`#0f172a`) to match real ink on optical crystal.
- Apply text shadows only when body copy crosses high-contrast underlying light patches: `text-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to an airy fluid grid where glass layers float with intentional breathing room, preventing dense clustering that compromises optical transmission.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive layout, `2.5rem` outer canvas margin, `1.5rem` column gutters. Maximum application container width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain focused focal planes.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid with `1.75rem` outer canvas margin, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `0.875rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Layering
- Internal padding within glass cards must remain generous (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to let edge specular reflections dissipate before text begins.
- Overlapping layers should stagger by at least `space-md` (16px) along horizontal or vertical axes to expose lower glass borders and refractive rim lines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through optical refraction, specular highlights, and calibrated transmission levels rather than heavy cast shadows.

### Glass Physics Model
Each elevated tier uses four distinct physical layers:
1. **Transmission Core:** `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(160%)` to preserve luminescence while softening underlying content.
2. **Beveled Specular Perimeter:** A 1px outer boundary that catches directional light:
   - `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
3. **Inner Optical Rim Highlights (Double Inset):**
   - Upper specular edge: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`
   - Lower refraction sink: `inset 0 -1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
4. **Refractive Dispersion Rim:** A micro gradient tint along the lateral edges running from `rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.08)` (cyan) to `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.06)` (indigo).

### Elevation Tiers
- **Surface 0 (Canvas Base):** Ambient pearlescent backdrop (`#f8fafc`) with subtle radial orbs of light.
- **Surface 1 (Base Panels, Tables, Workspace Glass):**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.50)`
  - Blur: `16px`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`
- **Surface 2 (Interactive Cards, Toolbars):**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`
  - Blur: `20px`
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Edge: Inner specular line intensified to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 1.0)`.
- **Surface 3 (Overlays, Modals, Spatial HUDs):**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.78)`
  - Blur: `28px`
  - Shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.10), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.05)`

## Shapes

The shape system expresses the curvature of polished optical glass lenses and prisms. Elements feature rounded profiles that guide perimeter specular reflections without visual pinch points.

### Radius Scale
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px). Matches the foundational curvature token.
- **Intermediate Surfaces (Cards, Menus, Dialogs):** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px). Balances panel definition with smooth corner refraction.
- **Dominant Containers & Modal Glass Windows:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px). Creates continuous flowing boundaries for spatial-grade elements.
- **Optical Circular Buttons / Floating Indicators:** Pure round (`9999px`) for touch targets, floating focal action buttons, and segmented control chips.

### Bevel Depth
Every shape features continuous 1px curvature tracking:
- Never apply jagged corners or hard rectangular cuts.
- Inner and outer nested radii must follow concentric geometry: child radius equals parent radius minus padding distance.

- **Secondary Refraction Button:**
  - Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.
  - Backdrop Blur: `12px`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`.
  - Text: `#334155`.
  - Hover: Fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`, border `rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.3)`.

### Input Fields
- Fill: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`.
- Backdrop Blur: `12px`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
- Inset Shadow: `inset 0 1.5px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- Text: Charcoal `#0f172a`, placeholder `#94a3b8`.
- Focus State: Border switches to `1px solid #0e7490`; refraction rim active with `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.15), inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.

### Cards & Panels
- Fill: Translucent gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35) 100%)`).
- Backdrop Filter: `blur(20px) saturate(150%)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
- Inset Specular: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9), inset 0 -1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- Refractive Trim: Lateral border edges tint delicately toward cyan-indigo via `background-origin: border-box`.
- Dynamic Hover: The card subtly lifts (`translateY(-2px)`), blur increases to `24px`, and the upper specular edge intensity doubles.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Geometry: Full pill radius (`roundedness: 3` equivalent, 9999px).
- Inactive: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`, border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`. Text `#475569`.
- Selected: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, border `#0e7490`. Text `#0f172a`, with a tiny `0.375rem` refraction dot indicator tinted `#0e7490`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Base Glass: 18px width/height, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`, border `1.5px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.5)`.
- Checked State: Fill transitions to `#0e7490`, displaying a crisp white micro-checkmark or center radio pip. Specular edge glow `0 0 10px rgba(14, 116, 144, 0.35)`.

### Lists & Segmented Groupings
- Containers house list rows separated by 1px frosted boundary lines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
- Row Hover: Smooth highlight sweep (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`) with rounded interior corners (`0.375rem`) within the card body.

### Optical HUD Pill (Spatial Status Bar)
- Suspended floating pill component docked to bottom or top center.
- Deep transmission: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, blur `32px`.
- Rim refraction: Pronounced dual-phase gradient highlight (`#38bdf8` to `#818cf8`) traversing the 1px top rim.
